Sticky Notes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app Sticky Notes & Reminders ("the app"), published on Google Play by Mossie Mostert ("we"), handles your information.

1. Summary

  • Your notes, photos and reminders are stored only on your phone. We do not run any servers and there are no user accounts.
  • We (the developer) cannot see, collect or sell your notes or photos.
  • The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications – to keep your notes one pull-down away and to show reminders.
  • Alarms & reminders (optional) – so a reminder rings on the exact minute you chose.
  • Run at start-up – to put your reminders and notes notification back after the phone restarts.
  • Internet – only to load ads.

Photos are taken with your phone's own camera app or chosen with Android's photo picker; the app only receives the one photo you pick and keeps a copy in its private storage. The app does not access your contacts, location, microphone or other files.

3. Sharing

When you tap Share on a card, the card's text and photo are handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or email). That only happens when you ask for it.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small banner on the notes screen and, at most a few times a day, a full-screen ad while you browse your notes. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, and how you interact with ads.

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change their choice at any time under the ⓘ (About) button > Privacy & ad choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Any data related to ads is handled by Google as described above.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Deleting your data

All notes, photos and reminders stay on your device. Deleted cards are kept in the app's trash for 30 days and then removed. You can remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
