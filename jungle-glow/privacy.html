Jungle Glow – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the game Jungle Glow ("the game"), published on Google Play by Mossie Mostert ("we"), handles your information.

1. Summary

  • The game runs on your phone. We do not run any servers and there are no user accounts.
  • Your progress, mangos, upgrades and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.

2. Permissions

The game uses internet access only to load ads, and vibration for game effects (which you can switch off in Settings). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional video ads that you choose to watch (for example to continue a level or double your mangos) and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, and how you interact with ads.

  • Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change their choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Any data related to ads is handled by Google as described above.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Deleting your data

All game data stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
