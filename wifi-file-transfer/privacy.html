WiFi File Transfer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the WiFi File Transfer app ("the app"), published on Google Play by Mossie Mostert ("we"), handles your information.

1. Summary

  • The app moves files directly between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi network. We do not run any servers and there are no user accounts.
  • Your files are never uploaded to us or to any cloud service.
  • The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.

2. Access to your files ("All files access")

The app asks for permission to access all files so that it can send and receive the photos, videos, documents and folders that you choose. Files are only read or written when you (or a device you connected with the one-time code) choose to transfer them. We do not collect, view or upload your files.

3. Camera

The camera is used only to scan the connection QR code shown on another phone. No pictures or videos are taken or stored.

4. Local network

To transfer files, the app runs a small file server on your phone that can only be reached by devices on the same Wi-Fi network, and only with the one-time code shown on your screen. Sharing switches itself off when not in use.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small banner on the home screen, an occasional full-screen ad after a transfer has finished, and an optional video ad that switches ads off for 24 hours. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, and how you interact with ads.

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change their choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The list of sent and received files is stored only on your phone. Any data related to ads is handled by Google as described above.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Deleting your data

All app data stays on your device. You can clear the "Received & sent" list inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
