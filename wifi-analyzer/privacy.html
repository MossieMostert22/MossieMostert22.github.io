WiFi Analyzer & Network Tools – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app WiFi Analyzer & Network Tools ("the app"), published on Google Play by Mossie Mostert ("we"), handles your information.

1. Summary

  • The app runs on your phone. We do not run any servers and there are no user accounts.
  • Scan results, speed-test history, device labels and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.

2. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location (precise and approximate): Android requires location permission before any app can scan for nearby WiFi networks or read cell-tower details. The app does not track, store or send your location.
  • Nearby WiFi devices / WiFi state: to scan access points, channels and signal strength.
  • Phone state: to read SIM, carrier and mobile-signal details on the Cellular screen.
  • Internet and network state: for the speed test, network checks, optional lookups and ads.

3. Online services the app contacts

These are used only when you start the related feature:

  • Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com, cloudflare.com, 1.1.1.1): speed test and network health check. Cloudflare receives your IP address as part of the connection.
  • ipify (api.ipify.org): shows your public IP address, only after you agree in the app.
  • OpenCelliD (opencellid.org): optional cell-tower lookup, only if you enter your own OpenCelliD API key. The cell identifiers of your serving tower are sent to look up its position. Your API key is stored encrypted on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad and an occasional full-screen ad after a test or scan finishes. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and general location, and how you interact with ads.

  • Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown and can change their choice at any time in Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or sell any personal information. Any data related to ads is handled by Google as described above.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Deleting your data

All app data stays on your device. You can remove it in Settings (clear history) or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
